About ARKA Logistics

Building the Future of Indian Logistics

A client-first, safety-first and technology-driven operating model.

Founded in July 2025, ARKA Logistics is building integrated transportation solutions spanning e-commerce fulfilment, freight, employee transportation and technology-enabled mobility.

Our long-term vision is to build a technology-enabled, safety-first and sustainability-focused logistics network across India — one that enterprises, e-commerce companies and corporate employee transportation clients can depend on.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide seamless and efficient logistics solutions that elevate employee travel experiences and contribute to our clients' overall success.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the logistics partner of choice, known for excellence, innovation and an unwavering focus on customer satisfaction across India and beyond.
